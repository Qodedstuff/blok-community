The Big Idea — Run the Server Like a TV Station
Strategy
🎬
Think of it like this: A TV station doesn't show just one programme all day. It has a schedule — morning show, afternoon news, evening entertainment, late night talk show. Each one attracts a different audience at a different time. That's exactly what we do for the Discord server. Different activities at different hours keep members from different timezones engaged all day long.

The Points System — Every Action Earns $BLOKC Seeds
Rewards
🌱
Think of it like this: Every time a member does something in the server — reacting to a Soil Report, sharing a Garden Watch, joining the Night Watch — they earn points called Seeds. At the end of each month, Seeds are converted to real $BLOKC tokens. The more active you are, the more you earn. It turns participation into real value.
Activity When 🌱 Seeds Earned
React to Soil Report Every hour +1 🌱
Post in Garden Watch Daily +5 🌱
Share Daily Harvest Every morning +10 🌱
Active in Night Watch Midnight +8 🌱
Refer a new member Any time +50 🌱
Plant in the protocol On-chain action +100 🌱
7-day activity streak Weekly bonus +200 🌱 BONUS
💱
Seeds → $BLOKC Conversion: At the end of each month, the team sets a conversion rate (example: 1000 Seeds = 10 $BLOKC). Seeds are burned and $BLOKC is distributed to wallets. This creates a direct link between community activity and real token value.

The Leaderboard — Who's the Top Bloktender?
Competition

It's like the school's top students getting a special badge, extra privileges, and recognition in front of everyone.
